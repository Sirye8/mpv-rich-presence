Connect AniList in mpv

Open the Rich Presence panel with Alt+p, choose AniList, then Connect account.

You approve access on AniList. This service exchanges the approval code and sends the result back to your player. It does not keep an account or token database. Your player stores the token in your OS credential store, or in memory for a session-only connection.

Playback, Discord activity and list updates do not pass through this service.
